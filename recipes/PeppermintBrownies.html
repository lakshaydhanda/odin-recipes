Peppermint Brownies

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients you will need to make delicious peppermint brownies:

For the Brownies:

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

For the Peppermint Frosting:

  • 1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream

Steps

Follow these steps to make peppermint brownies:

For the Brownies:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x13-inch baking pan.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the melted butter and granulated sugar. Mix well.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract to the butter-sugar mixture and beat until smooth.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.

For the Peppermint Frosting:

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the cocoa powder, melted butter, powdered sugar, vanilla extract, and salt. Gradually add the heavy cream and mix until smooth and creamy.
  2. Pour the brownie batter into the prepared baking pan and spread it evenly. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  3. Allow the brownies to cool completely in the pan before spreading the peppermint frosting on top. Slice into squares and serve.
