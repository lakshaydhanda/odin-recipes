Shorecook's Chocolate Peppermint Biscotti

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients you will need to make delicious Shorecook's Chocolate Peppermint Biscotti:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup crushed peppermint candies

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, beat the eggs, vanilla extract, and peppermint extract until well combined.
  4. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
  5. Stir in the chocolate chips and crushed peppermint candies.
  6. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto ungreased baking sheets.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the edges are firm.
  8. Allow to cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
