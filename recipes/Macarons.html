Macarons

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients you will need to make delicious macarons:

  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix almond flour, powdered sugar, and cornstarch.
  3. In another bowl, whisk together coconut oil, milk, and vanilla extract.
  4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until smooth.
  5. Pipe the batter onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the macarons are set.
  7. Allow to cool before serving.
