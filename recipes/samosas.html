Samosas

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients you will need to make delicious samosas:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 cup boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1/4 cup boiled peas
  • Oil for frying

Steps

Follow these steps to make samosas:

  1. Mix the flour, water, and salt to make a stiff dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds, turmeric powder, garam masala, and red chili powder. Fry for a few seconds.
  3. Add the boiled and mashed potatoes and peas to the pan. Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Roll out the dough into small circles and cut them in half to make semi-circles.
  5. Place a spoonful of the potato mixture in the center of each semi-circle.
  6. Bring the edges together to form a cone shape and seal the edges with water.
  7. Fry the samosas in hot oil until they are golden brown.
